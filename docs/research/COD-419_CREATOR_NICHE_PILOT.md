# COD-419 — permissioned creator niche pilot preparation

**State: No-Go for recruitment, real-data collection and execution.** This is a research protocol and draft participant material, not an approval to process X content. COD-419 remains blocked by COD-369's real archive/rights decision and the applicable X use permissions. Creator consent cannot override X developer terms. See the [COD-372 protocol](X_DISCOVERY_DISTRIBUTION_MVP.md#cod-372--permissioned-niche-comparison-protocol-and-synthetic-evaluation), [archive feasibility](X_ARCHIVE_FEASIBILITY.md), and [security guide](../security.md). Do not let this optional research delay a safe personal-only V1 pilot.

## Preregistered cohort and screening

**Candidate niche:** independent English-language software development educators who publish original X posts about practical programming or developer tools. This is a convenience sample, not a claim about all X creators. Recruit only after the entry gates below pass, through opted-in developer educator communities, direct introductions and a public research invitation that does not scrape or harvest handles. Record channel, invitations, replies, eligible/ineligible counts and nonresponse; avoid selecting only high-performing accounts.

Target at least **12 eligible creators**, each with **30 compatible, non-repost observations** and at least **five classified strong, five typical and five weak** examples. Recruit above 12 to allow exclusions, but do not lower the threshold. Predeclare follower count at the first eligible publication as the audience-size proxy: **1,000–9,999** and **10,000–49,999**; evaluate each band separately and require enough compatible peers within a band. Do not pool bands just to reach the minimum. English only for this first protocol; record multilingual posts as exclusions. Creator-declared niche is confirmed by a blinded review of a fixed, chronological sample of recent posts, not by outcomes. Require at least 30 eligible posts in a rolling **180-day publication window** and at least four original posts in the last 30 days at screening; this limits stale-history bias. The 30 observations need compatible metric snapshots, not merely 30 published posts. Exclude accounts with unverifiable ownership, unavailable consent, paid/promoted posts, automated or predominantly repost feeds, substantial unrelated topics, missing timestamps, inconsistent metric source/definition, or no compatible observation window. Do not infer demographics.

Screen in this order: (1) record invitation channel and a random private candidate ID; (2) ask the questions below without requesting an archive; (3) verify account control by an approved private authenticated owner flow or a time-limited challenge placed by the owner and removed afterward, with no password or token shared with researchers; (4) review the minimum post/metric coverage counts only after rights clearance and consent; (5) apply fixed exclusions and record reasons; (6) lock the roster and strata before inspecting outcome differences. A creator who declines a purpose is excluded from that purpose. A second researcher checks ambiguous eligibility without seeing outcome values.

**Screening questions (draft):** Do you control the X account and can you prove it by the approved flow? Do you primarily publish original English posts about software development education? Approximately how many followers did you have during the proposed period? How many original posts did you publish in the last 180 and 30 days? Are posts promoted, automated, or routinely reposted? Can you provide a permitted source with exact publication and metric observation timestamps for the fixed window? Are you willing to allow private, revocable research comparison of deidentified metric and format records with other consenting creators? Which data uses below do you decline? Do you need an accessible consent format or have questions? Answers are preliminary and never replace source verification.

## Participant materials — drafts, do not send yet

**Short invitation:** “BipMe is planning a small, opt-in study with software development educators on X. You can decline or withdraw. We are not recruiting or collecting data yet; this is a draft invitation. If the study opens, we will send a full information sheet and ask for separate consent.”

**Information sheet:** The study compares two research recommendations on historical, held-out posts: personal history alone and personal history plus eligible peer patterns. It cannot promise reach or explain X's ranking. Participation is voluntary and has no effect on BipMe access. The proposed source is an owner-supplied, field-limited export or approved official owner-history access; the final source and exact field list must be approved before invitation. Researchers would keep account verification and contact details apart from pseudonymous comparison rows. Only authorized research staff can access identifiable records. Other participants will not see your posts, handle, raw counts or identity. Results may be reported only as aggregate counts and limitations, with small-cell suppression reviewed before release. Risks include reidentification from uncommon patterns and unauthorized disclosure; controls and residual risk must be explained during approved consent. There is no guaranteed direct benefit or payment in this draft. Contact, controller, jurisdiction, retention duration and withdrawal route must be filled and approved before use.

**Plain-language consent checklist (separate affirmative choices; no prechecked boxes):**

## Data specification and collection controls

Freeze one metric before collection. **Proposed primary measure:** count of replies to an original post at **48 hours after publication, ±2 hours**, unit `count`, definition “X-reported public reply count at capture; cumulative; excludes inferred/private replies.” This is a hypothesis, not a claim that archives contain that snapshot. A single late archive counter is **not** a 48-hour measurement. If no permitted source yields real age-aligned snapshots, stop and choose a newly preregistered protocol; do not relabel late totals. Impressions may be recorded only if specifically permitted and genuinely observed at the same age, with exact definition and access provenance; they are optional context, never imputed, never pooled as raw reach across creators, and absence does not become zero. Freeze source, capture method, metric, period, 48-hour window, pattern taxonomy and size band before outcome review. Format pattern is a prepublication descriptor such as `text_only`, `image`, `video`, or `link`; determine it from permitted metadata, without post text or outcome leakage.

One private, access-controlled source register maps an opaque random `creator_key` to the verified account and consent. A separate minimal observation table contains: opaque `post_id` (unique within pilot), `creator_key`, `niche`, `audience_size_band`, `language`, `pattern`, `metric`, `metric_definition`, `window`, `value` (nullable nonnegative integer count), `published_at` and `observed_at` (UTC with original timezone retained in audit metadata), `owner_verified`, `comparison_permission`, and `evidence_provenance` (source class, capture version/time and field provenance, no handle or raw path). The private register also records actual source post ID, permission version, recruitment channel, deletion state and access log. Preserve a per-source completeness manifest and counts of omitted fields. Never treat download/import time as a metric observation time. Deduplicate source post IDs and snapshots; select one predetermined capture per post in the window. Require `observed_at >= published_at`, finite nonnegative metric, exact window match and no observation after its evaluation cutoff. Keep missing values explicitly null with a reason. Record deletions/unavailable posts and exclude them rather than silently retaining stale evidence.

Collection, if later approved, happens in a private creator-scoped staging area using the Platform-owned source route; no new endpoint is authorized here. Accept only allowlisted post history and metric fields, reject DMs, contacts, credentials and unrelated archive entries, and do not commit raw material or put it in GitHub/Linear. Archive format and post/metric coverage must first be proven on a permitted sample per COD-369. Restrict access to named researchers on least privilege, encrypt storage and transfer, separate the identity key from comparison data, log access and exports, prohibit external model processing, and document incident response. Set a short project-specific retention period and backup expiry **before** intake; schedule source destruction after validation and derived-row destruction at pilot close or withdrawal, whichever is earlier. Test withdrawal with synthetic records across source, derived rows, cached reports, exports and backups, and audit that the withdrawn creator cannot enter future runs. No real collection until the exact schedule and deletion proof are approved.

## Evaluator handoff and analysis procedure

The merged `NicheEvaluationPost` and `NicheEvaluationSpec` dataclasses in `apps/api/src/bipme/intelligence/permissioned_niche_evaluation.py` define the field mapping above. Set spec `target_creator_key`, `niche`, `audience_size_band`, `language`, `metric`, `metric_definition`, `window`, `expected_observation_age_hours=48`, `observation_age_tolerance_hours=2`, and a fixed `as_of`; retain the defaults of eight warm-up posts, three peer contributors and one to two sampled examples per band unless a new protocol is approved. Set `selection_basis="voluntary"`. Do **not** feed real rows to the current function: it explicitly requires `synthetic-` IDs and `synthetic://` provenance and returns `synthetic_only=True`. Replacing real identities with synthetic-looking strings to bypass that guard would mislabel real results. A future, separately reviewed research-only adapter must preserve real provenance in a private audit trail and reuse the tested matching, banding, equal-creator weighting and walk-forward logic without claiming the synthetic harness processed real data. No adapter is built by this issue.

Preflight validation reports counts by creator, band, stratum, source and exclusion reason. Require 12 eligible creators with 30 compatible observations each and five **time-respectingly classified** posts in each band; raw quantiles of full history do not establish these counts. If a band or size stratum lacks enough peers at a fold, abstain and report coverage. Use the complete eligible chronological frame, including weak and typical posts, not just visible winners. Lock train/holdout cutoffs and pattern taxonomy before reading target outcomes. At each target publication cutoff, use only earlier published posts whose outcomes were observed by that cutoff; classify against each creator's earlier available history. Freeze both personal-only and personal-plus-niche recommendations, then score the later exact-window target against its own earlier creator-relative P25/P75. Report eligible/scored folds, recommendations, matched-target denominators, coverage, modal pattern stability across predeclared cutoff and creator resamples, mean matched band score and its incremental difference. A matched-only score can be selection-sensitive, so report unmatched cases and abstentions prominently. Run 3×IQR exclusion replay and report when IQR is zero/not identifiable. Bootstrap **creators**, not posts, for a 95% interval; with only 12 creators, treat intervals as exploratory and do not declare effectiveness. Run leave-one-creator-out and predeclared size-band checks. Preserve source isolation and do not expose another creator's rows in output.

## Execution gates and runbook

1. **Rights/privacy clearance — STOP now.** Record written source-specific decisions for archive/official access, cross-creator analysis, retention, derived data, display, embeddings and any provider transfer; resolve COD-369's current archive sample/format gap and COD-344/COD-375 rights. Approve controller/contact, consent version, access roles, incident process and retention schedule. Test synthetic withdrawal/deletion end to end. Do not recruit before this gate.
2. **Recruitment.** Publish only the approved invitation through the stated channels. Log all invitations, refusals and recruitment sources without outcome-based selection.
3. **Screening/verification.** Apply the fixed questions, ownership check, niche/activity/size/language criteria and exclusion log; independently review borderline cases. Stop if the 12/30/5-per-band minimum is unattainable.
4. **Consent/collection.** Capture separate affirmative choices and versioned scope. Receive only the approved fields through the approved private route; collect no real content under this preparation PR. Stop intake on rights, security or deletion failures.
5. **Dataset validation.** Verify IDs, provenance, UTC timestamps, exact metric definitions and observation ages, source completeness, missingness, duplicate/deleted posts and compatible strata. Publish aggregate exclusion counts and lock the dataset manifest.
6. **Time-respecting evaluation.** Preregister cutoffs and resamples, run the authorized research adapter once on the untouched holdout, keep personal and peer arms separate, perform outlier and creator-clustered sensitivity checks, and retain reproducible code/version hashes without publishing data.
7. **Interpretation.** Report recruitment funnel, sampling bias, denominators, coverage, stability, score difference, interval, size/language strata, unavailable impressions, withdrawals and limitations. Ask participants for structured usefulness feedback only with separate approved consent; do not claim causal reach uplift.
8. **Decision.** Complete the template below. A 12-creator pilot decides only whether a larger study is feasible. A limited V1 niche comparison requires the separate, preregistered **30-creator** holdout and all COD-372 thresholds. Otherwise retain personal-only reporting with niche evidence labeled unavailable/insufficient.
